Elsewhere

Privacy Policy · LyfEscapes LLC · Effective November 7, 2026

Elsewhere is a maze game by LyfEscapes LLC ("we"). This policy explains what happens to information when you play Elsewhere on Android.

What we collect

Nothing. Elsewhere has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics and no servers. The app does not even ask for permission to use the internet, so it cannot send anything anywhere. We never see your name, email, contacts, photos, files, location or any identifier for your device.

What stays on your phone

Which levels you have unlocked and your best number of actions on each level are stored only on your device, so the game remembers your progress. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes them.

Google Play

You download Elsewhere from Google Play. Google may collect information about installs and crashes under Google's Privacy Policy; that is between you and Google, and we only see anonymous totals in the Play Console.

Children

Elsewhere does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes we will post the new version at this address with a new effective date.
