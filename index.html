Elsewhere

Privacy Policy · LyfEscapes LLC · Effective November 9, 2026

Elsewhere is a maze game by LyfEscapes LLC ("we"). This policy explains what happens to information when you play Elsewhere on Android.

What we collect

Nothing. Elsewhere has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics of our own and no servers of ours. We never receive your name, email, contacts, photos, files, location or any identifier for your device.

What stays on your phone

Which levels you have unlocked, your best number of actions on each level, your view and sound settings, and whether you bought "Remove ads" are stored only on your device, so the game remembers them. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes them. A purchase can be restored afterwards with the Restore button.

Ads (Google AdMob)

From version 1.1, Elsewhere shows ads from Google AdMob: an optional video you can choose to watch for a hint, and an occasional full-screen ad between mazes. The game itself still works offline; the internet is only used for ads and purchases. To choose and measure ads, prevent fraud and improve its service, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects information from your device and shares it with Google and its advertising partners, including:

  • your device's advertising ID and app set ID,
  • your approximate location, estimated from your IP address,
  • app interactions such as launches and ad views, and
  • diagnostic information about app and SDK performance.

This information goes to Google, not to us. It is encrypted in transit. How Google uses it is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and some US states), the app asks for your consent before personalised ads, and you can change your choice at any time with the Privacy button on the title screen. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's Android settings.

Purchases (Google Play)

Elsewhere offers one optional in-app purchase, "Remove ads", which removes the between-maze ads and makes hints free. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play under Google's Privacy Policy; we never see your payment details. The app only learns from Google Play whether you own the purchase.

Google Play

You download Elsewhere from Google Play. Google may collect information about installs and crashes under Google's Privacy Policy; we only see anonymous totals in the Play Console.

Children

Elsewhere is meant for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices and deletion

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to delete. To remove what is on your phone, uninstall the app or clear its storage. To limit ad data, use the Privacy button in the game or the ads settings on your phone.

Changes

If this policy changes we will post the new version at this address with a new effective date.
